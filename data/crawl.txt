EPISODE XII: RETURN OF THE EWOK

It is a dark time for the league. Twelve franchises have battled 11 seasons for the title of Supreme Chancellor. Now a small, furry army has amassed at the edge of the league.

The Angry Ewoks, once a punchline, now stand at the doorstep of dynasty status. Endor was just the beginning.

Elsewhere in the galaxy, old empires stir. Dark Helmet plots yet another rebuild. The Jar Jar Binks list claims new victims each season. And somewhere, an owner is once again only "one piece away"...
